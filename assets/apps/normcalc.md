# Normal Distribution Calculator

## Probability from Score

* Enter the mean ($\mu$) and standard deviation ($\sigma$) of the normal distribution $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$
* Enter the value of the raw score you, $x$, for which you want to find either $P(X<x)$ or $P(X>x)$
* Click calculate

The app will give you the probability that a value is less than and more than the raw score entered.

## Raw Score from Probability

* Enter the mean ($\mu$) and standard deviation ($\sigma$) of the normal distribution $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$
* Enter the probability for which you want less than a raw score
* Click calculate

The app will give you the raw score, $x$, for which the probability of being less than this is equal to the entered probability.
