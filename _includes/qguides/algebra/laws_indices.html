# Rules of Indices
## Multiplying Algebraic Expressions When two single term algebraic expressions are multiplied, the coefficient part is multiplied and the algebraic parts are written next to each other. #### Example Simplify $5x \times 3y$ The coefficients multiply to $5 \times 3 = 15$, and so: $$ 5x \times 3y \equiv 15xy $$
## Rules of Indices The rules of indices are: $$ \begin{align*} x^m \times x^n & \equiv x^{m+n} \\[8pt] x^m \div x^n & \equiv x^{m-n} \\[8pt] \left (x^m \right )^n & \equiv x^{mn} \end{align*} $$ Some other key points regarding indices: $$ \begin{align*} (xy)^n & \equiv x^ny^n \\[8pt] x^1 & \equiv x \\[8pt] x^0 & \equiv 1 \end{align*} $$
### Some Examples #### Example 1 Simplify $$4x \times 8pq$$ Here multiply the $4$ and the $8$ and write the rest: $$32xpq$$ Note, it is conventional to write the numerical part first, but the alegraic parts can be written in any order here. #### Example 2 Simplify $$6x^3y \times 4x^2y^3$$ Multiplying the coefficients gives $24$. Using the multiplication rule of indices for the $x$ variable gives $x^3 \times x^2 \equiv x^{2+3} \equiv x^5$ For the $y$ part, $y \times y^3 \equiv y^{1+3} \equiv y^{4}$ Therefore: $$24x^5y^4$$ #### Example 3 Simplify $$\frac{3rs^2 \times \left (2r^4s^{-2} \right )^3}{12r^2s}$$ Looking at the coefficient part you have $3 \times 2^3 \div 12 = 2$ Look at the $r$ part you have $r^{1 + 4 \times 3 - 2} = r^{11}$ Looking at $s$ part you have $s^{2 + (-2) \times 3 - 1} = s^{-5}$ Putting it all together: $$2r^{11}s^{-5} $$
