## Body Mass Index (BMI)

<div class="instructions" markdown="1">

The formula for the **body mass index (BMI)** is

$$\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{mass (kg)}}{\text{(height (m))}^2}$$

</div>

#### Question 8

## Body Surface Area (BSA)

<div class="instructions" markdown="1">

The formula for the **body surface area (BSA)** in $\text{m}^2$ is

$$ \text{BSA (m}^2) = \sqrt{\frac{\text{height (cm)} \times \text{mass (kg)}}{3600}}$$

</div>

#### Question 9

$$\sqrt{2}=1.41 \quad \sqrt{3}=1.73 \quad \sqrt{5}=2.24 \quad \sqrt{6} = 2.45 \quad \sqrt{7} = 2.65$$

Find the body surface area, BSA, in m$^2$ of the following people. Only enter the numerical value correct to 2 decimal places.

## Ideal Body Weight (IBW)

<div class="instructions" markdown="1">

Use this formula for IBW

$$
\begin{align}
\text{Female: IBW (kg)} &= \text{height (cm)} \times 0.9 - 92 \\[8 pt]
\text{Male: IBW (kg)} &= \text{height (cm)} \times 0.9 - 88
\end{align}
$$

</div>

#### Question 11

For a person who is:

**Male; Height = 6 ft; Mass = 14 stone 4 lbs**

Find, using the conversions given in the earlier part of this workshop:

{% include question_numerical.html

    question_text="Their body surface area (BSA) in m$^2$, correct to 2 decimal places."

    solution_text="$$ 6 \text{ ft} = 6 \times 12 \text{ in} = 72 \text{ in} = 72 \times 2.5 \text{ cm} = 180 \text{ cm}$$

$$ 14 \text{ stone } 4 \text{ lbs} = 14 \times 14 + 4 \text{ lbs} = 200 \text{ lbs} = 200 \div 2 \text{ kg} = 100 \text{ kg}$$

$$ \text{BSA} = \sqrt{\frac{180 \times 100}{3600}} = \sqrt{\frac{18 \times 10}{36}} = \sqrt{\frac{1 \times 10}{2}} = \sqrt{5} = 2.24 \text{ m}^2$$"
%}
