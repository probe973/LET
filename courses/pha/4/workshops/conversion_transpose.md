$$x=\frac{y-c}{m}$$"

{% include question_rearrange.html
    id="pha4w02q5d"
    title="5d"
    question_text="Make $x$ the subject of the formula $y-10=3(x-2)$."
    correct_answer="(y-4)/3"
    solution_text="Expand the brackets on the right:
$$y-10 = 3x-6$$

Add $6$ to both sides:

$$y-4 = 3x$$

Divide both sides by $3$:

$$x=\frac{y-4}{3}$$"
    var_label="x="
%}
